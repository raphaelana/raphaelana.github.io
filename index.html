Raphael Anaadumba

PhD Student in Computer Science · University of Massachusetts Lowell
Advisor: Prof. Mohammad Arif Ul Alam · CUBICS Lab
NSF Award #2230180 · $2.5M

I build machine learning systems that make hidden environmental and health risks visible — transforming fragmented, incomplete, and privacy-sensitive public data into actionable risk maps that help cities protect their communities.

My primary focus is Graph Neural Networks for spatial public health, with applications to lead contamination in water infrastructure and early disease screening from non-invasive audio signals. My work is aligned with EPA Lead and Copper Rule Improvements (LCRI, 2027 enforcement) and designed for deployment in resource-limited environments.

I have published 6 peer-reviewed papers as first or co-author at AAAI, IEEE/ACM CHASE, ICASSP, Frontiers in Environmental Engineering, and Nature Scientific Reports. I serve as a certified peer reviewer for Springer Nature journals.

Open to collaborations and internship opportunities. Whether you are a researcher, peer, or industry lab seeking a motivated PhD collaborator in GNN, spatial ML, or health AI — please reach out at atiimanaadumba200@gmail.com.

Research

  • Environmental health AI — lead contamination, water safety

  • Telehealth and edge-device deployment
