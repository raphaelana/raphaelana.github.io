Questions, comparisons, findings

What does the graph actually add?

My work tests the contribution of relational structure in clinical signals and environmental-health records. These examples show the comparisons behind the results.

01 · Clinical audio · First author, ICASSP 2026

Which relationships help detect cognitive impairment from speech?

I connected Audio Spectrogram Transformer embeddings of overlapping 10-second speech windows using temporal adjacency and acoustic similarity. The ablation asks whether each edge type improves prediction beyond pooled window representations.

TAUKADIAL: mild cognitive impairment classification
RepresentationAccuracyAUROC
AST only51.3%0.474
AST + temporal edges80.5%0.645
AST + temporal + similarity edges81.8%0.728

Finding: Temporal adjacency accounted for most of the accuracy gain. Similarity edges added less to accuracy while improving ranking, as measured by AUROC.

Speaker-disjoint, stratified 10-fold cross-validation. Models were trained and evaluated separately on Pitt and TAUKADIAL; this is within-cohort evaluation. The graph component adds approximately 0.47 million parameters, about 0.5% of the AST backbone.

02 · Physiological acoustics · First author, ACM/IEEE CHASE 2025

Graph modeling beyond speech

I developed attention-based CNN–GCN models for respiratory disease classification from STFT representations of lung sounds. The ICBHI dataset contains 1,021 recordings from 126 patients, with expert annotations for crackles and wheezes.

Contribution: Combining convolutional signal features with graph-based aggregation extends my clinical-audio work to respiratory acoustics.

The prediction task is disease classification. Crackle and wheeze annotations describe the recordings; the study does not report separate event-localization performance.

03 · Environmental health · Ongoing research

When pooled performance conceals a site shortcut

In Massachusetts lead-risk modeling, a pooled AUROC of 0.78 concealed AUROCs of 0.34–0.62 within individual cities. I traced the discrepancy to features encoding city identity, including a covariate whose association reversed between pooled and city-level analysis.

Response: I rebuilt evaluation per city so that aggregate performance could be assessed alongside performance within each site.

An ongoing analysis, reported in my résumé. These results do not establish transfer to unseen cities.

04 · Dissertation · Work in progress

How does the value of graph structure change when labels are scarce?

I study how graph topology and label availability affect relational learning in population-scale risk prediction. Alongside self-supervised pretraining, I am developing graph-conditioned latent diffusion and evaluating whether synthetic records reduce the number of real labeled examples needed for prediction.

The generation work is under development. The published self-supervised study provides a starting point for this question.
